## Short description

Absorb light to heal and grow in a twin-stick arena shooter. Choose a new hull, break the weapons off towering elites, and explore a world of rival AIs. Damage can shrink your ship. The next evolution is another chance to change.

## About this game

Begin as a small white core inside a light-blue seed hull. Every light circle you absorb restores the same bar that lets you evolve. Push too hard and damage takes your latest shape away; survive, gather light and choose a different future.

**Become what you consume.** Fire, Corruption, Plasma, Lightning and Void offer different arrangements and attacks. Grow through five tiers and choose among three complete hull builds at each evolution. Follow another element's light to change the options you receive.

**Break the weapons off.** Large elites carry several independently firing weapon circles. Destroy those circles to silence attacks, release light and expose the core. Read the shape, find an opening and keep moving.

**Choose your direction.** Explore a procedural world whose angular regions determine the light you find and whose distance determines the danger. Every node exit remains open, even during a rival fight. Push toward five rival cores to finish the campaign, then continue exploring.

**Reboot and return.** If your light runs out, return to the origin with a seed hull. Your discoveries, reached waypoints, unlocked elements and beaten cores remain. Rebuild and jump from the origin to a waypoint your current tier can handle.

Planned feature copy for final review:

- Five elements and 81 player hulls across five tiers.
- One light bar for health, growth and regression.
- Fixed hull builds with distinct primaries, secondaries and passives.
- Destructible elite weapons and decision-driven rival encounters.
- Free node-to-node escape, persistent waypoints and five core objectives.
- Keyboard/mouse and controller control paths, rebinding and auto fire.
- Save/resume and compatible demo progress import.

## Demo description

Explore Fire, Corruption and Plasma regions, grow through three tiers and challenge the Fire core eight nodes from the origin. Continue exploring after its defeat, or import compatible progress into the full campaign.
