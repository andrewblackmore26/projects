# Lightship — unpublished store draft

Review copy for the v0.3 game. The working title, price, release date, languages beyond English and minimum hardware remain owner decisions. Windows and Linux are target platforms; final package, physical-controller and Steam account qualification must precede public feature claims.

## Short description

Absorb light to heal and grow in a twin-stick arena shooter. Choose a new hull, break the weapons off towering elites, and push through five bounded levels of rival AIs. Damage can shrink your ship. The next evolution is another chance to change.

## About this game

Begin as a small white core inside a light-blue seed hull. Every light circle you absorb restores the same bar that lets you evolve. Push too hard and damage takes your latest shape away; survive, gather light and choose a different future.

**Become what you consume.** Fire, Corruption, Plasma, Lightning and Void offer different arrangements and attacks. Grow through six tiers and choose among three complete hull builds at each evolution. Follow another element's light to change the options you receive.

**Break the weapons off.** Enemies are graphs of independently destructible circles, not one hitbox. Large elites carry several independently firing weapon circles — destroy them to silence attacks, release light and expose the core. Read the shape, find an opening and keep moving.

**Push outward.** Each of five levels is a bounded region of connected nodes around a starting point; one boss always waits on its sealed far edge. The whole level's map fits on one screen, with the boss's direction and distance always known. Every node's open connections stay open, even mid-fight — camping a node earns steadily less than moving on.

**Reboot and keep flying.** If your light runs out, the next life starts immediately, in a freshly laid-out world — no loading, no confirmation. Your discoveries, unlocked elements and beaten level bosses remain.

**A voice between fights.** A companion reacts to your first evolution, your first setback, your first elite, each new element you unlock, and every level boss you meet and defeat — always between fights, never interrupting one.

Planned feature copy for final review:

- Five elements and 101 player hulls across six tiers.
- One light bar for health, growth and regression.
- Fixed hull builds with distinct primaries, secondaries and passives.
- Destructible elite/boss weapon circles and decision-driven rival encounters.
- Five bounded levels, one boss per level, and a level map that always shows the boss's bearing.
- Momentum-based flight with a dash and a warp between nodes; a reduced-warp accessibility option.
- Keyboard/mouse and controller control paths, rebinding and auto fire.
- Save/resume and demo progress import into the full campaign.

## Demo description

Explore Lightning and Fire — the first two of the full campaign's five levels — with no cap on how far your hull can grow. Beat the second level's boss to reach the demo's ending, or keep exploring. Import your demo progress into the full campaign from the main menu at any time.
